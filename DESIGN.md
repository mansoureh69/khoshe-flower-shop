## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the warmth, sensory delicacy, and artisanal prestige of a high-end Persian boutique floristry. It reconciles traditional Iranian botanical heritage with contemporary editorial minimalism. The experience addresses discerning clients seeking bespoke floral arrangements, curated gifts, and ceremonial styling for memorable milestones.

### Design Movements & Tone
- **Editorial & Tactile Minimalism:** Generous breathing room, precise hairline dividers, and subdued organic surfaces evoke the feel of an art publication or luxury lookbook. The chrome steps aside so the botanical subjects capture focus.
- **Organic Persian Warmth:** Rather than clinical cool greys or stark high-tech blacks, all tones emerge from floral anatomy—deep botanical greens, rose petals, dried sage, and warm parchment alabaster.
- **Atmospheric Restraint:** Restraint governs visual noise. Color is utilized structurally rather than decoratively; interactive pops are isolated and protected to convey quiet prestige.
- **Dual Script Harmony:** Persian typography (RTL) sits at identical optical gravity alongside Latin display accents, respecting Persian calligraphy curves while preserving high-legibility digital ergonomics.

## Colors

The palette derives strictly from live floristry and classical botanical illustration: deep evergreen foliage, soft rose petals, sage underleaves, and aged ivory vellum. Pure digital black (`#000000`) is strictly barred from all UI surfaces and typography.

### Role & Color Allocation
- **Primary (`#1F3A2B` - Botanical Forest):** Anchors brand authority, luxury crests, active navigation signposts, and deep editorial containers. Conveys botanical richness and longevity.
- **Secondary (`#B5485F` - Rose Petal):** The singular interactive accent. Reserved strictly for primary action buttons, key order CTAs, selection highlights, and pricing accents. Viewports must contain at most one dominant rose CTA to preserve visual hierarchy.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A059` - Champagne Gold):** Expresses heirloom quality, artisanal seals, specialty botanical tiers, delicate ornamental frames, and rating highlights.
- **Neutral (`#241E1C` - Carbon Ink):** High-contrast, warm dark brown used for headings and primary body copy, achieving WCAG AAA contrast against ivory without harsh digital pitch.

### Surfaces & Accents
- **Base Canvas (`#FDFBF7` / `#FAF7F2`):** Delivers a warm, natural linen quality that avoids screen glare and flatters floral photography.
- **Sage (`#5F7054` / `#E9EDE3`):** Semantic positive feedback, stock readiness, fresh dispatch badges, and organic reassurance indicators.
- **Blush (`#F6E9E5` / `#EFDAD4`):** Soft foundational fill for chips, code highlights, notice callouts, and category backdrops.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances luxury display flourishes with effortless transactional clarity.

### Script & Font Pairings
- **Display Headlines:** Set in `Playfair Display` for Latin contexts, paired symmetrically with high-contrast Persian serif typefaces (`Peyda` or `Vazirmatn Display`) for Persian RTL text. This provides classic letterforms, distinct counter-spaces, and traditional floral booklet heritage.
- **Interface & Reading Body:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` for Latin numerals and English micro-copy, and `Vazirmatn` for Persian copy. The open counters, balanced ascenders, and geometric clarity ensure legible product labels, ingredient lists, and delivery checkout details.
- **Persian RTL Typography Rules:** 
  - Persian line-heights require 10–15% more vertical clearance than Latin equivalents to prevent overlapping ascenders and descenders (sar-kash and dot clusters).
  - All numerals within prices, delivery times, and inventory levels render with Persian tabular figures (`persian-tabular-nums`) to prevent shifting layouts during cart updates.
  - Section labels and category eyebrows maintain generous tracking in Latin, but use natural kerning in Persian to preserve script connectivity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, low-opacity ambient shadows tinted by the natural ink tone (`rgba(36, 30, 28, ...)`). Digital grey or harsh drop shadows are strictly avoided.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base backgrounds (`#FDFBF7`) and tinted sections (`#FAF7F2`) sit flush with hairline dividers (`1px solid #E4DCD5`).
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Tiles):** `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(36, 30, 28, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(36, 30, 28, 0.03)`. Applied to product cards, testimonial tiles, and segmented selectors against ivory backgrounds.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Floating Elements):** `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(36, 30, 28, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(36, 30, 28, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a smooth `-2px` transform on product cards.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-over Cart & Drawers):** `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(36, 30, 28, 0.16)`. Backdrop overlay uses ivory-tinted darkness `rgba(36, 30, 28, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`.
- **Top Navigation Bar:** Sticky header with `rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.88)` tint, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, and bottom border `1px solid #E4DCD5`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a refined organic geometry: structured card corners combined with full pill rounds on touch interactions.

### Shape Tiers
- **Soft UI Containers (`8px` to `12px`):** Product card bounds, imagery containers, and cart summary containers utilize `12px` (`var(--r-md)`) corners. Form fields, dropdown menus, and text areas utilize `8px` (`var(--r-sm)`) corners for tailored structural composure.
- **Pill Geometry (`999px`):** Buttons, category selector chips, stock status pills, and counter tags utilize full pill radiuses (`rounded-full`), reflecting petal curves and thumb-friendly mobile targets.
- **Circular Targets (`50%`):** Floating cart triggers, add-to-cart "+" actions, wish-list badges, and decorative floral stamps maintain a 1:1 circular aspect ratio.
- **Aspect Ratios:** Product images use a fixed vertical **4:5** ratio to complement stem length and bouquet proportions; editorial banners use **16:9** or **21:9**.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Affordances
- **Primary CTA:** Full pill (`rounded-full`), filled with Rose (`#B5485F`), text in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`). Hover state transitions to Rose Deep (`#8E3548`). Enforces a minimum tap target of `44px` height with `24px` horizontal padding. Only one primary Rose button is rendered per visual hierarchy tier.
- **Secondary CTA:** Full pill, outlined with Botanical Forest (`1.5px solid #1F3A2B`) or Carbon Ink, background transparent, text in Ink (`#241E1C`). Hover introduces a blush surface wash (`#F6E9E5`).
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, underlined or icon-accompanied ink text, shifting to Rose text on hover.

### Product Card (`pcard`)
- Built on a pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a subtle hairline border (`1px solid #E4DCD5`) and `12px` border radius.
- Imagery framed at 4:5 ratio with gentle corner rounding (`8px`) inside card padding (`12px`).
- Features a floating circular `+` button (`36px × 36px`) in Rose (`#B5485F`) anchored to the bottom corner of the photo block.
- Persian product nomenclature set in Bold Body (`16px`), accompanied by stem or flower variety in muted ink (`13px`), and prices rendered in tabular figures (`#1F3A2B`, bold).

### Filter & Category Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `36px` height, horizontal padding `16px`.
- Inactive state: White or Alabaster fill with hairline border (`#E4DCD5`), text in Soft Ink (`#5C5450`).
- Active / Selected state: Filled in Forest Green (`#1F3A2B`) or Carbon Ink with White typography, deliberately reserving the Rose tone for commerce checkouts.

### Form Inputs & Date Pickers
- Background filled with Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), `8px` corner radius, `1px solid #E4DCD5` border.
- Active focus state: `2px solid #B5485F` with `0 0 0 3px rgba(181, 72, 95, 0.12)` halo.
- Persian date selector optimized for Solar Hijri (Jalali) calendar selection with delivery time-slot pills.

### Status Badges & Pills
- In-stock / Fresh delivery: Sage Soft background (`#E9EDE3`) with Foliage Sage text (`#5F7054`).
- Limited edition / Artisan: Blush Soft background (`#F6E9E5`) with Rose text (`#B5485F`).
- VIP / Signature arrangement: Champagne Gold Light background (`#EFE6D5`) with Champagne Gold text (`#8F6F27`).
